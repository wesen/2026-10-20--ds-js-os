# PicoCalc compiler research

Start with the [compiler research index](ttmp/2026/11/19/PICOCALC-COMPILER-THESIS--cold-compilation-and-bounded-analyses-on-the-picocalc/reference/02-picocalc-compiler-research-index-and-open-directions.md). It maps the professor’s native roadmap to experiments, production changes, and open questions.

The [eight-study supervisor report](ttmp/2026/11/19/PICOCALC-COMPILER-LAB2--eight-experiments-in-compiler-construction-on-the-picocalc/analysis/01-what-a-compiler-can-afford-on-the-picocalc.md) gives on-device cold compilation, real edit-to-native-return timings and generated-code measurements. Its [ticket](ttmp/2026/11/19/PICOCALC-COMPILER-LAB2--eight-experiments-in-compiler-construction-on-the-picocalc/index.md) links every study, its reproduction scripts and its investigation diary.

The [independent studies](ttmp/2026/11/19/PICOCALC-RV32-STUDIES--independent-picocalc-compiler-studies-of-branch-lowering-and-output-lifetime/index.md) measure RV32 branch generation and native output allocation. The [newcomer report](ttmp/2026/11/19/PICOCALC-RV32-STUDIES--independent-picocalc-compiler-studies-of-branch-lowering-and-output-lifetime/analysis/01-how-a-handheld-compiler-spends-time-and-memory.md) explains the physical results, the host-to-device difference, and the next integration experiment. The [combined integration trial](ttmp/2026/11/19/PICOCALC-RV32-COMBINED--qualify-branch-fusion-with-compact-output-in-the-streamed-picocalc-compiler/analysis/01-what-happens-when-two-native-compiler-optimizations-share-the-same-output.md) tests both changes together across Files, Agenda and Editor. The production baseline streams typed functions on the ESP32-P4. The [integration guide](ttmp/2026/11/19/PICOCALC-COMPILER-INTEGRATION--integrate-qualified-compiler-optimizations-into-the-picocalc-toolchain/design-doc/01-production-integration-of-the-typed-compiler-optimizations.md) describes the compiler entry points and the buffering option.

The [first four-study compiler program](ttmp/2026/11/19/PICOCALC-COMPILER-LAB--measure-cold-compilation-and-test-bounded-compiler-designs-on-the-handheld/index.md) covers cold frontend costs, RISC-V instruction generation, bounded analysis and function-level recompilation. Each study has its own design, evidence and diary.

The [four-study supervisor report](ttmp/2026/11/19/PICOCALC-COMPILER-LAB--measure-cold-compilation-and-test-bounded-compiler-designs-on-the-handheld/analysis/01-which-compiler-work-pays-for-itself-on-a-handheld.md) explains physical Editor cold builds, reservation size, bounded proofs and the Files edit-to-native-return measurement. Its [published vault edition](https://github.com/go-go-golems/go-go-parc/blob/5e62fa498c37bf5480f2f6c1cf338fc918166916/Projects/2026/11/19/PROJECT%20REPORT%20-%20PicoCalc%20Compiler%20-%20Which%20Compiler%20Work%20Pays%20for%20Itself%20on%20a%20Handheld.md) links all five individual reports.
