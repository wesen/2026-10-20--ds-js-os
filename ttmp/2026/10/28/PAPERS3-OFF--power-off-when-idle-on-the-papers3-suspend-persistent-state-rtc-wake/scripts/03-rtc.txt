# P3: the RTC switches the board on. Armed for 2 minutes, the board goes off and comes back by itself; the
# clock driver reports the wake reason and clears the flags, so a later power-off stays off until the button.
wait 2000
send off off {"inMin":2}
wait 60000
print a minute later: still off
wait 70000
print the RTC switched it on
send off off
wait 600000
print ten minutes later: still off (the timer was stopped at boot)
power
wait 2000
# on USB the board stays on, and an armed timer power-cycles it when it runs out (as the device does)
battery 100 usb
send off off {"inSec":20}
wait 25000
print the timer ran out while the board was on
wait 2000
