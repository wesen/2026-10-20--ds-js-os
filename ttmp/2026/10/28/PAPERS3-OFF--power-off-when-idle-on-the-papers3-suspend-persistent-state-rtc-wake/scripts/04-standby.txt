# P4: standby on the full system. The REPL is on screen with a line evaluated; a minute without touch on battery
# suspends: the REPL saves, the compositor draws the asleep screen, the board goes off. The power button boots it
# into the REPL with its lines. On USB with the cell full the power-off fails and the system resumes. With
# wakeEveryMin the RTC starts the board, which goes back off 20 s later.
battery 80
wait 3000
send compositor open {"app":"repl"}
wait 1500
send repl type {"text":"6*7"}
send repl key {"arg":{"key":"enter"}}
wait 1500
send standby config {"idleMin":1}
capture 40-before.png
wait 61000
capture 41-asleep.png
wait 5000
power
wait 4000
capture 42-resumed.png
battery 100 usb
send standby now
wait 6000

battery 80
