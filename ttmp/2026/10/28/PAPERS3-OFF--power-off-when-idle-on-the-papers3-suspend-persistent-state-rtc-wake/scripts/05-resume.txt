# P5: resume without a flash. The REPL on screen, idle for a minute: the asleep screen (one clean refresh), saved.
# After the power button the first present is a partial "text" update of what changed (the title bar's clock
# and battery, the footer), not a clean refresh. Then a resume after a boot with no saved picture (the record
# is used once) flashes as before.
battery 80
wait 3000
send compositor open {"app":"repl"}
wait 1500
send repl type {"text":"6*7"}
send repl key {"arg":{"key":"enter"}}
wait 1500
send standby config {"idleMin":1}
wait 62000
panel
capture 50-asleep.png
power
wait 4000
panel
capture 51-resumed.png
capture 52-resumed-clean.png 2 clean
send compositor paintcheck {}
tap 270 500
wait 2000
send compositor paintcheck {"report":true}
