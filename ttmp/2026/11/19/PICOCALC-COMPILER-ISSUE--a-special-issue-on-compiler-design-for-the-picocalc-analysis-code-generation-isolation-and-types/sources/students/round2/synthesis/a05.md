- **`bump`, model A: "15" does not follow from the stated rules (1/3: s1).** With 5.2's entry and exit (4 and 3) they give 17 or 18. The script gets 15 because a model A leaf saves no return address ("leaf: no ra save"), a rule the article omits.
- **"11 to 14 percent of today's instructions" (5.7) (1/3: s3).** The 5.3 rows subtract to 11.9, 13.3, 14.9. The record shows why: "All frame loads and stores, including locals" also counts arguments stored at entry.
- **"one instruction, `blez a, after_if`" (2/3: s2, s3).** In 5.2 `a` is in the frame at offset 8, so it is `lw` plus `blez`.
- **5.7 "the seven temporaries `t0`–`t6` cover almost all code" against 5.8 "sets aside 5 of them for temporaries in a leaf" (1/3: s3).**
- **"one of three things" (5.5), then "a fourth kind of entry" (5.6) (1/3: s1).**
- **"take them in order" (1/3: s1):** the rows map to 5.5, 5.7, 5.4, 5.6.
