## 1. Errors and inconsistencies in the article

Confirmed:

- "by about a thousand where only part of an application is typed" (3/3). True only for optimized CJI against WebAssembly (206/0.20 = 1,030; 523/0.52 = 1,006). Other pairings give 726 to 2,837. Name the pairing.
- "not all of the 182 KiB difference is code" (3/3). 182 KiB is memory used (710 − 528); the compiled sizes differ by 152,197 bytes (148.6 KiB), leaving about 33 KiB. Two differences are mixed; no quantity is given.
- "the longest time one uninterrupted piece of a turn may run" (1/3) implies a turn has pieces; "A **turn** is the handling of one event" does not.
- Grader's own: "Eight terms, set in bold below" opens a section with 14 bold terms; "Three terms are needed to describe the engines" precedes five.
- Grader's own: the glossary says the manifest lists engines and turn budgets; the article's sentence on the manifest omits both.

Not confirmed: s1's contradiction between the 37 ms and the compile-time table; s2's flash arithmetic.

## 3. Passages to rewrite

- "Between 58 and 169 times slower than a laptop core" (3/3): which measurement gave which end?
- "It may also do anything the firmware may do." (2/3): "it" is far from "the native version".
- "`text.length` is a load of the 32-bit count stored at the start of the string" (2/3): the layout is asserted, never shown.
- "the reactor owns a fixed set of them and lends them to the program" (2/3): how many, and where does `countUnit`'s `text` come from?
- "must save its progress, ask for a timer, and continue" (2/3): why the budget exists (s2) and what saving involves (s1) are missing.
