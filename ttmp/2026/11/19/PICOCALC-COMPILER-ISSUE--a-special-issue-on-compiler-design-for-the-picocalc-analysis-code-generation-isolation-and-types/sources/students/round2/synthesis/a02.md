## 1. Errors and inconsistencies in the article

Confirmed:

- "the whole WebAssembly path, measured as one piece, took 29.9 ms" (2/3). The table gives 28.5 ms; the 1.3 ms link, declared "not part of that total", makes 29.8. The contents of 29.9 ms are unstated; the factor of 66 rests on it.
- "58 percent frontend, of which tokenizing alone is 16 percent" (2/3 saw a sum of 99). 28.5/48.7 is 58.5 percent; 20.2/48.7 is 41.5. Tokenizing is 28 percent of the frontend (grader).
- The glossary's "Token" entry is a split-phase number; "16,792 tokens" is lexical (2/3).
- Section 2.6 demands "at least ten times"; "about 340 ms in each case" rests on "three boots", no spread (2/3).
- The CJI rows sum to 1,480.8 s, not "1,480 s in total" (1/3).
- Grader's own: "application code under 5 percent of a key press" holds for native code only; 8.8 ms gives 8.8/44.4, 20 percent, for WebAssembly.
- Grader's own: in Article 1's diagram a key goes from keyboard driver to application; here "the compositor received a key" first.
- `_assets/handheld-compile-phases.png` is missing from `issue-round2/` and the students' directories (s2).

Not confirmed: that "upper limit" applies to the share but not to 1.045 (s2).
