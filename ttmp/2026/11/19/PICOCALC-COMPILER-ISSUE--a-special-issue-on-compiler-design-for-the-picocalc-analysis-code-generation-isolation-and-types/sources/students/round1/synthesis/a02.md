## 1. Errors and inconsistencies in the article

Checked against the article and, where named, outputs in `various/`.

- **The per-token cost is wrong (3/3).** "the tokenizer alone costs about half a second per boot, and the source has 16,792 tokens, so each token costs about 118 microseconds, or roughly 42,000 processor cycles". Half a second over 16,792 tokens is 31 µs (about 11,000 cycles); 118 µs is the whole 1,988 ms compile per token. The cycle figure needs the 360 MHz clock, which only Article 1 gives (3/3).
- **"29.9 ms" cannot be derived (3/3).** "the WebAssembly path that takes 29.9 ms here": tokenize + rest of frontend + link is 29.8 ms; with the evaluation row, 32.2 ms. `04-compile-phase-timing.txt` shows 29.85 ms is a separate median and the rows are differences of medians.
- **The shares lack a denominator (3/3).** 16%, 42% and 41% hold only over 48.7 ms, excluding the 2.4 ms evaluation; over 51.1 ms they are 16, 40, 40. No total row.
- **Second compositor trace (3/3).** "of 235 ms, 84 ms was drawing and 146 ms was handling other messages": 84 + 146 = 230; 235 ms is also unexplained beside the table's 293 ms.
- **"all 161 functions" (1/3, s2).** `03-register-cache-model.txt` lists 120 functions for Editor, 34 for Agenda, 7 for Files; the next sentence speaks of Editor alone.
- **"More than half of the emitted instructions" (1/3, s2),** repeated in 2.8: Files is 50.0% (764 of 1,529).
- **Key points firmer than the body (1/3, s2).** 2.8: "application code is under 5 percent of a key press"; 2.2 calls its numbers "not enough to act on". 2.8 also joins device time with workstation counts: "88 percent of its instructions in one analysis".
- **Two points from s3 (1/3 each).** Of the "four kinds of interval", the fourth "is a correctness check". 1.6 ms is per turn; 2.2 uses it per key press.
- **Found while checking (0/3).** 2.8's "roughly evenly between its frontend and RV32 lowering" holds only for "Rest of the frontend" (42% against 41%); with tokenizing the frontend is 58%. Article 1 gives Agenda's original CJI compile as 1,475 s; here the frontend alone is 1,479.1 s.
