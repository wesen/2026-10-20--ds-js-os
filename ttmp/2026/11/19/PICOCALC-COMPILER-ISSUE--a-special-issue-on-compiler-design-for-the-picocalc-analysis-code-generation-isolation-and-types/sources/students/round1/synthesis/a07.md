## 1. Errors and inconsistencies in the article

Each item was checked.

- **Fuel accounting overclaims (s1, s2).** §7.5: "the amount subtracted at each back-edge can be the length of the loop body". With an `if` or a nested loop the count depends on the path; only an upper bound is static. Typed functions called from the body are never charged, so "The counter then bounds executed instructions" does not follow.
- **Stack formula ambiguous (3 of 3).** §7.4: "the largest sum of frame sizes along any chain of calls, plus the stack needed by the deepest reactor function that chain can reach". Is the reactor term maximised per chain or separately? "Deepest" is undefined.
- **Verifier claims hold only with masking (grader's check).** §7.6: pattern checking "still depends on the compiler having kept types straight". Yet the trust table drops the compiler, and the §7.7 table answers "Protects against compiler errors" with "yes" beside "0 to 2 instructions".
- **Wrong cross-reference (grader's check).** §7.6: "for reasons established in Article 5. The backend uses about twenty different instructions." Article 5 gives only the 4-byte size. The count appears nowhere, and trampolines only in Articles 10 and 11.
- **Return address unprotected (s1).** "loaded from the frame slot where the function's entry saved it" does not exclude a store overwriting that slot.

Not confirmed: the §7.4 diagram is correct (`helper` precedes `scan`); s2 and s3 misread it.
