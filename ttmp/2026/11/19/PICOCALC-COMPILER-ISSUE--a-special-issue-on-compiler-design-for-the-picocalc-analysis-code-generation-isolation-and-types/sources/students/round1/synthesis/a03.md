## 1. Errors and inconsistencies in the article

- **`max` has no value (3/3).** "computed `(max + 2) - max` as 2 where JavaScript gives 1". Confirmed in Node: JavaScript gives 1 only for `max = 2**53 - 1`; for `2**53` it gives 2, as s2 found.
- **Six rounds, seven analyses (3/3).** "ran the complete range analysis after every round" gives six; the table says "Complete range analyses | 7".
- **Three totals for one build (2/3).** "the 25 minutes", "1.2 s of 1,480 s", "1,475 s → 544 s". Article 2's profile gives 1,480 s, Article 1 gives 1,475 s; the table mixes them.
- **The constraint list omits `k` (1/3).** "the type of `x` includes whatever `area` passes and whatever `half` passes" has no counterpart for `k`, the parameter the example turns on. s2's own repair, "`0.5 → v → k`", is wrong (`v` flows to `x`): the passage misleads.

- **Miscounts (*grader*).** "Four measurements" introduces five table rows. "Three earlier systems" introduces four entries, and the key point "Self, RPython and the Cartesian product algorithm" drops Shed Skin.
- **11,760 ÷ 95 = 123.8 (1/3).** "Visit" is undefined.
