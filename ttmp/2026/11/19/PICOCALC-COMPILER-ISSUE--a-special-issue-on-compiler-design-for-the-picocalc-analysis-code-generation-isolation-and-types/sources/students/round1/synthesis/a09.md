## 1. Errors and inconsistencies in the article

- **Bounds-check cost, §9.4 (3/3; verified).** "seven instructions without the check and would have nine or ten with it" makes a check two or three instructions. The Cost line says "each removed check saves about four instructions under the improved backend of Article 5". Nothing reconciles them, and Article 5 gives no per-check cost.
- **Which calls end a guard fact, §9.4 (2/3: s1, s3; verified).** The rule says "a call is made that could change the length"; the Cost line says "cleared at assignments and calls". If only some calls clear the fact, the compiler needs facts about callees, which criterion 1 excludes.
- **"both branches must have consumed the same handles", §9.6 (2/3: s2, s3; verified ambiguous).** Is revoking in one branch and storing in the state in the other accepted? The preceding sentence implies yes. It cost s3 a quiz point.
- **Grader's checks, not raised by students.** The introduction promises "seven additions"; there are six sections (§9.2–9.7) and six rows in §9.10, so seven holds only if typestate is counted separately. §9.11 shortens criterion 2 to "costs nothing at run time", yet the arena row lists "2–3 instructions per object".
