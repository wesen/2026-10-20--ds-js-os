# Independent scientific review

The results support a release trial for late lowering of the two checked text operations and for streaming frameless leaves. Late lowering produced the largest measured fresh-compiler gain on Chess. Frameless lowering reduced the time to finish the same Chess search work across five configurations. Their joint effect on the player’s first frame and response to a move remains an integration question. I reviewed the stored receipts, recomputed the physical comparisons and replayed the frozen stock host oracle. The physical experiments belong to the original investigators. [Director synthesis](../analysis/07-what-a-handheld-compiler-should-remember.md) · [Review diary](21-review-diary.md)

## What the clocks measured

| Experiment | Replication and timing boundary | Independent result |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh Editor compiler | Four complete six-arm blocks in one experimental boot. Start immediately before creating a fresh QuickJS runtime, end after returning the linked bytes to C and disposing of the runtime. | Stock 4.545 s, all-late 4.164 s. Paired median reduction 8.685%, observed range 7.851–9.304%. |
| Fresh Chess compiler | Same image and limit. All Chess blocks preceded the Editor blocks. | Stock 5.342 s, all-late 3.038 s. Paired median reduction 43.017%, observed range 42.583–43.919%. |
| Native telemetry | One boot, 11 blocks per endpoint. One warm-up replay per arm, then elapsed time for 48 replays of 11 inputs including loop overhead. | All-late saved 4.982% on parse, 15.901% on count and 21.267% on label relative to stock. |
| Native Chess search | Five fixed configurations, three matched repetitions per configuration. Timed search initialization and successive slices through completion. | Frameless reduced median paired time 6.857–7.793%. The 1,600-node medians were 239.159 ms stock, 221.526 ms frameless and 220.738 ms with inlining. |

Each percentage compares the policy and stock in the same block, then takes the median of those fractional changes. The repetitions share a device, a boot, input data and a deterministic rotating arm schedule. The ranges describe observed variation within the experiment. The 48 inner replays are work accumulated for one clock reading. On telemetry the two identical-code controls gave zero median paired change for all three endpoints. Comparing **selected minus all-late directly** gives a median of 0 µs for parse (range −7 to +7), 0 µs for count (−1 to +1) and 10 µs for label (+9 to +11) per timed batch. The exceptionally close stock-relative parse percentages provide little basis for ranking those two policies on that endpoint. [Telemetry timings](../various/director/physical.json) · [Warm-up, order and checks](../scripts/director/board/main/bench.c) · [Chess search timings](../various/03-leaf/physical.json)

The fresh-compiler probe preloaded the app source and reactor once per world. Its clock included compiler JavaScript reads and evaluation, lowering, linking, native output copy and QuickJS teardown. The four-block comparison uses 48 of 59 successful records. The missing sixtieth was Editor’s fifth bounds-selected observation. Five stock/all-late pairs survived for each app. Their paired medians, 9.031% on Editor and 43.094% on Chess, preserve the conclusion. Peak JS bytes measure the high-water mark of **live memory charged to the QuickJS allocator**. The median fell by 77,846 bytes on Editor and 1,237,062 on Chess. The C output buffer and other firmware allocations have separate lifetimes and accounts. [First capture](../various/01-bounds/physical-first.json) · [Probe caller](../scripts/01-bounds/board.c) · [Timer and allocator](../../../../../../picocalc-os/port/esp32p4/main/typed_p4.c)

Two production differences affect the interpretation. The ordinary Editor and Chess callers start the clock before reading **both** application assets and use a 6 s limit, compared with 9 s for the probe. Both allow 4 MiB of JS heap. The experimental image also loads its modified, multi-policy compiler bundle for the stock arm. For execution, the dedicated native probes place machine code in flash-mapped text. The playable profile loads each native ELF into PSRAM. Comparing two flash addresses with identical stock bytes checks those placements. Qualification in the ordinary loader will measure the change in instruction memory, data placement and app scheduling together. A verified restore establishes the firmware state after collection. ## Decisions by study

1. **Bounds admission.** Reject the two current budgets as selectors for the observed opportunities. On current Chess, visiting all 137,531 cells removed one check and 32 bytes from `chessMake`. Its 1,139 cells exceed the entire 640-cell allowance. The unlimited arm took 6.885 s on Chess, 28.54% longer in same-block comparisons. The separate, stronger engine on an older Editor found one 16-byte saving in a 263-cell function, larger than the selected policy’s 192-cell ceiling. The positive repeated-read fixture established that the transformation can pay when the compiler finds a repeated check. The independent telemetry had zero removable checks even under unlimited admission. [Chess admission ledger](../various/01-bounds/chess-admission-ledger.json) · [Current release results](../various/01-bounds/host.json) · [Older Editor](../various/01-bounds/older-editor.json)
2. **Checked operations.** Trial all-late with the ordinary compiler. It won on fresh compile cost and label writes and generated the smallest telemetry image among the individual semantic policies. The intervention changes both frontend representation and the backend instructions used for a guard or literal write. Only an additional matched lowering control could isolate the contribution of retaining operation identity. Allocation-site or garbage-collection attribution would likewise require separate instrumentation. [Treatment definitions and missing controls](../analysis/02-checked-operation-lifetime.md)
3. **Leaves and composition.** Prioritize the streaming frameless arm. The inlining arm also buffers function bodies and added 76 bytes over stock on telemetry. For the full suite I verified exact additivity of modeled instruction savings on this one program. Twelve full-app host links show Editor and Chess instruction extents shrink 15.0% and 29.5% under late/frameless. The Editor and Chess ELF sizes stayed 242,124 and 274,892 bytes. Use the ordinary loaded world and matched search work for the release runtime decision. [Chess experiment](../analysis/03-leaf-lowering-and-selective-inlining.md) · [Composition](../various/director/composition.json) · [Full release-world links](../various/director/composed-release-worlds.json)
4. **Fragment reuse.** The retrospective clean fragment comparison corroborates all six release-app body, signature and layout reuse counts. Each of the four valid held-out edits changed only one fragment. The fifth changed the arity of a called function and reproduced the clean compiler’s diagnostic after seven cache hits. Retaining the old generation allowed a subsequent unchanged replay to reuse all ten. For physical edit latency the planned timer encloses the resident compiler call returning an ELF. Cache-session setup, publication, subsequent world loading and the first native response are outside it. The retained cache’s size and whole-app response time each need a separate observation. [Clean-fragment oracle](../various/04-deps/clean-fragments.json) · [Held-out edits](../various/04-deps/held-out.json) · [Device edit timer](../scripts/04-deps/09-board.js)
5. **Stack certificates.** Uphold the registered stop decision. Host monitoring reached the predicted conditional peak on the five invoked telemetry entries, including 64 bytes for `readBurst`. Two accepted instructions outside the registered policy invalidate its zero-false-admission criterion. The metadata also exceeded the prescribed bound on both apps. No measured stack overrun is needed to decide that gate. External stack consumption remains outside the typed-only bounds. [Policy audit](../various/05-stack/policy-audit.json) · [Held-out depth measurements](../various/05-stack/held-out.json)
6. **Turn lifetimes.** Keep the inference prototype scoped to the complete world validator and its declared SDK behavior. Of eight independent scenarios, three safe and two unsafe cases were expressible and correctly classified, and three required a durable reference absent from the language. One safe development case received a conservative rejection. In a separate direct-persistence example the low-level compiler accepted a program which the complete linker rejected because world state fields must be scalar. Equal native bytes and modeled resume results support the specific accepted examples. [Verdicts and runtime observations](../various/06-lifetimes/runtime-and-cost.json) · [World validation boundary](../various/06-lifetimes/world-boundary.json)

The ten-function telemetry source was held out when the policies were chosen. The first two researchers locked decision rules before disclosure, while the other four also supplied implementation identities. The 1,603 differential cases, including 101 traps, strengthen the check on this program. The subsequent crossed-arm test reuses the same program and was conceived after the individual results. This memorandum covers the completed first-run receipts above. The fourth board window for physical composition and the dependency study’s physical edit timings were still pending at review.
