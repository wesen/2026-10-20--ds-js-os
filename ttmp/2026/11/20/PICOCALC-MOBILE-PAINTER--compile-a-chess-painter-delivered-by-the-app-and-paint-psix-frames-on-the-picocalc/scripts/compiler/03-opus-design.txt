Act as an independent compiler researcher for a generic embedded systems experiment. You have no tools and no access to the implementation. A small game publishes typed painter source once from an active process to a receiver. The receiver compiles a closed first-order subset to RV32 and keeps the executable entry; later PSIX-style frames carry a validated, bounded game snapshot. Native code emits fixed drawing records into owned scratch, and a clipped native compositor paints and presents the frame. For a board game with 64 tiles, pieces, cursor, destination marks and small status lines, propose a minimal record ABI, obligations at install and per-frame boundaries (distinguish real isolation from ordinary buffer checks), and a physical qualification matrix. Include what timings and correctness checks establish the source actually traveled and was compiled only on installation. Give a directed approach to disconfirm the experiment. No analogies. Return a concise technical research note.
