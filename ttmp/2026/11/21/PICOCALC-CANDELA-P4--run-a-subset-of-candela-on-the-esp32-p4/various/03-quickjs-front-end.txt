- All presets: 105/105 build in QuickJS with one shim (atob, for K-09); generated WGSL and diagnostics are byte-identical to Node's for all of them.
- Limits a world would hit: Candela alone needs about 1,375 KB charged (a world's default limit is 1,024 KB: import fails); C stack up to 69 KB in 32-bit (S-04 over the P4's 56 KB), deeper in 64-bit.
- QuickJS-specific: no atob; `//# sourceURL` is ignored, so diag.js callSite() cannot see user frames (<input>:line:col) in a bundled build.

For scale only, not a device measurement: the nucleus calibrates QuickJS on the ESP32-P4 at 133 ms for its
100k loop and 31 ms for fib(20) (nucleus/vm.c). In 32-bit QuickJS here they take 3.04 and 0.93 ms of CPU,
ratios 44x and 33x. The GGX table (3,680 ms here) would scale to about 123-161 s;
B-01 with the table memoized (2.02 ms cold, 2.01 ms warm here) to about 67-88 ms cold.
The table is double-precision float work; the P4 has no double-precision FPU, so if anything it scales worse than both.
